# AB & Associate - Premier NFC Digital Business Card & Service Portal

An ultra-luxurious, state-of-the-art NFC Digital Business Card and Service Showcase website built for **AB & Associate** (Tax, Accounting, Advisory, Compliance) based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 💳 Interactive 3D Virtual NFC Card
- Realistic **3D Flippable card** with metallic gold sheen reflection and high-contrast typography in both Dark and Light themes.
- Mouse parallax 3D tilt on desktop and gyroscope tilt on mobile.
- Chip visual, contactless NFC indicator, and dynamic QR Code on back face.

### 2. 🌐 3-Language Multi-Lingual Switcher
- **English (EN)**
- **ગુજરાતી (GU)** (Gujarati)
- **हिंदी (HI)** (Hindi)
- Instant full-page translation of all 7 service categories, bullet points, headers, forms, and badges without page reload.
- Remembers user language preference using `localStorage`.

### 3. 🌗 Luxury Dark & Light Themes
- **Dark Mode (Default)**: Royal Midnight Navy & Brushed Gold Foil accents with glassmorphism.
- **Light Mode**: Crisp Executive White & Slate with high-contrast text and warm bronze gold accents.
- Smooth instant toggle with `localStorage` persistence.

### 4. 📇 1-Tap Save to Contacts (vCard .vcf)
- Generates and downloads a complete `AB_and_Associate_Contact.vcf` card with full name, direct phone (+91 99745 74539), email (abassociate039@gmail.com), location (Ahmedabad), note, and website.

### 5. 📂 7 Comprehensive Service Suites (with Search & Category Filters)
1. **Income Tax Services** (ITR Filing, Tax Planning, Notices, Appeals, TDS, 26AS/AIS, Refunds, Tax Assessment)
2. **GST Services** (Registration, Returns, Nil Filing, Revocation, Annual Return 9/9C, ITC Reconciliation, Notice Replies, E-Invoice)
3. **Accounting & Bookkeeping Services** (Bookkeeping, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, BRS, Receivables/Payables, MIS Reports)
4. **Company & MCA Compliance** (Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC Incorporation, Annual Filings, Director KYC, Registers, Strike-off)
5. **Business Advisory** (Startup Structuring, Financial Planning, DPR & CMA Data for Loans, Compliance Review)
6. **NGO / Trust / Society Services** (Registration, 12A/12AB, 80G Tax Exemption, Form 10B, CSR Documentation)
7. **Other Professional Services** (PAN, TAN, DSC Class 3, Professional Tax, MSME/Udyam, FSSAI, Trademark)

### 6. 📱 Interactive Utilities & Redesigned Components
- **Quick Action Bar**: 1-Tap Call, WhatsApp, Email, and Google Maps Navigation.
- **Consultation Booking Modal**: Prefills service and generates structured WhatsApp inquiry.
- **Compliance Due Dates Calendar**: Live tracking of key tax deadlines (GSTR-3B, GSTR-1, TDS, Advance Tax, ITR, MCA).
